Advertising and analytics choices

The game uses Google UMP to request advertising choices where configured. Ad requests in the release version depend on whether UMP reports that ads may be requested. Settings can open ad privacy options when the Google form is available. The availability and content of regional messages depend on the final AdMob and UMP dashboard configuration, which is not yet verified.

Gameplay analytics is a separate choice. The game checks device-side SIM/network country, device region and time zone, and a Google consent region signal. If a signal indicates the European Economic Area, United Kingdom or Switzerland, or the region cannot be determined, analytics remains off until you opt in. In another determined region, the game shows a first-launch notice and offers opt-out before sending events. These device signals are used to choose the prompt; the application does not send those region-check results as analytics events. This behavior alone does not establish legal compliance in every country.

Turning analytics off stops future gameplay event collection and clears local pending events and checkpoints; it does not automatically erase events already delivered to Google. Enabling it later does not send earlier gameplay retrospectively. The analytics configuration disables collection by default, analytics use of the Android advertising ID, and ad-personalization signals. This does not mean the AdMob SDK uses no identifiers. See Data deletion for separate device and provider actions.

The applicable recipient roles, countries, transfer mechanisms and safeguards must be confirmed against the actual release services and terms before this draft can be published. You may ask us for information about safeguards relevant to your data.

How long data remains

Local progress, settings, hints and local transaction records remain on your device until changed by the game or removed with its storage, subject to device backup behavior. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to answer and document the request and meet any applicable legal or claims obligations; a concrete schedule must be confirmed. Google Play purchase records, ad and consent records, GitHub logs and any Google Analytics events follow their respective service settings and obligations. The actual Google Analytics retention setting and deletion controls have not been verified, so this draft makes no fixed-month promise. See each provider's policy for its own retention rules.

Your rights and requests

Where GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. We will respond without undue delay and generally within one month; if a request is complex or numerous, GDPR permits an extension of up to two further months with notice and reasons within the first month. Rights can have legal exceptions and identification limits; we will explain an applicable limit. Use the contact address above. You may complain to the Portuguese Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) or another competent supervisory authority.

Required data, automated decisions, and security

Local game state is needed to retain progress. Billing information is needed only if you choose to purchase; advertising and analytics choices affect their respective services. You do not need to provide a name or email to play, but an email is needed if you contact support. The game does not use gameplay analytics to make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects for you. Ad providers may use automated ad selection or profiling under their own policies. We limit the game data sent for gameplay analytics and use the security controls provided by the app stores and services; security and release settings still require verification.

Children

The current game text says it is not intended for children under 13. This is a provisional audience statement, not an age verification or parental consent system. Age of consent and youth advertising rules vary by location; the release audience and settings must be reviewed before publication.
