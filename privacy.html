Who is responsible

Hidden in the Canvas is made by Dmitry Rukhlenko, an individual developer based in Portugal, who is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. His full contact details, including postal address and telephone, are listed in the Terms of Use. For privacy questions and requests, write to hiddeninthecanvas@omail.sh .

Some of the services the game uses work on our behalf, and others decide for themselves how they use data:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase processes gameplay analytics for us as a processor, under the Google Ads Data Processing Terms .
  • Google AdMob and Google's consent service (User Messaging Platform) are run by Google as a separate controller for the advertising data they collect.
  • Google Play is a separate controller for your Google account, payments and purchase records.
  • GitHub hosts these pages and handles visitor logs under its own privacy statement.
  • Our email provider hosts the support mailbox for us.

Advertising and analytics choices

Where the law requires it, Google's consent message asks for your advertising choices before ads are requested, and the game requests ads only when Google's consent service reports that it may. Depending on your choices, Google may show personalized, non-personalized or limited ads. You can review or change these choices at any time in Settings with Manage Ad Consent. Where Google offers a form in your region and the consent service can be reached, it opens Google's consent message if a choice is still required, or Google's privacy options once a choice is on record. When Google's form closes successfully, ads preloaded under your earlier choices are discarded; the game requests new ads only when Google's consent service reports that it may, and Google applies your updated choices to those requests. Remove Ads stops the game from requesting or showing ads between levels; rewarded ads for hints stay available because you choose to watch them.

Google's ad services may use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your Android settings (usually Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads), which also limits personalized ads in this and other apps. This is an Android setting; it is not a separate opt-out under the privacy laws of individual US states. We do not currently offer a dedicated in-app US state privacy opt-out control. You can contact hiddeninthecanvas@omail.sh with a privacy request.

The game does not ask for your age. It is designed for adults and may be used from age 16. We configure Google Mobile Ads with a maximum ad content rating of PG for every ad request the game makes, so ads rated for teen or mature audiences are not requested.

Gameplay analytics is a separate choice, controlled in Settings under Gameplay analytics. To choose the first-launch prompt, the game checks the SIM or network country, the device region and time zone, and a regional signal from Google's consent service. If any of them points to the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or the region cannot be determined, a first-launch prompt asks whether to turn analytics on, and analytics stays off until you do. In other regions, the game shows a notice on first launch that lets you turn analytics off before any events are sent. Both prompts say that we receive this data through Google Analytics for Firebase with an app installation ID, and link to this policy. The game does not send the results of this region check as analytics events.

Turning gameplay analytics off stops future gameplay reporting, clears pending analytics data on your device, and resets the Analytics ID. Events Google has already received follow the retention and deletion rules below. We configure gameplay analytics so that it does not collect the Android advertising ID and does not use the data for ad personalization. These settings cover analytics only; advertising follows your choices in Google's consent message.

Recipients and transfers

Data is received by the services that run the game and its support: Google (Google Play, AdMob, the consent service and Google Analytics for Firebase), our email provider, and GitHub for these pages. Through AdMob, advertising partners may also receive data for ad delivery and measurement. Where Google's consent message applies, it lists these partners and lets you accept or reject them; you can see the list and change your choice later in Settings with Manage Ad Consent. Google and GitHub may process data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Both are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has found to provide adequate protection, and they also use the Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Advertising partners listed in Google's consent message and our email provider may also process data outside the EEA; where they do, they rely on the transfer safeguards described in their own privacy policies, such as adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards or for a copy of them.

How long data remains

Progress, settings, hints and local purchase records stay on your device until the game changes them, you reset them, or you clear the game's storage or uninstall it. Android backups may keep a copy according to your device's backup settings.

We keep support emails for 24 months after your request is resolved. We keep them longer only when we need them to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. Google Play purchase records, advertising and consent records, and GitHub logs follow each provider's own retention rules and legal obligations.

Our Google Analytics settings retain event-level data for two months and user-level data for 14 months. New activity restarts the retention period for the analytics installation identifier. These limits apply to detailed data; aggregate reports may remain available longer. See Google Analytics data retention .

During development we exported test events to BigQuery in the EU. We have ended that export. The earlier test tables are set to expire 60 days after creation. Google Cloud may retain recovery copies for a limited period after expiration.

Your rights and requests

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where it applies, and you may object to processing based on our legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing: turn gameplay analytics off in Settings, or change your advertising choices with Manage Ad Consent. We respond without undue delay and within one month. If a request is complex, we may extend this by up to two more months; we will tell you why within the first month. Some rights have legal exceptions, and we can only act on data we can link to you; we will explain any such limit. Write to the contact address above. You may also complain to the Portuguese Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) or another data protection authority.

To request deletion of gameplay analytics data, open Settings, scroll to Privacy and support, and choose Request data deletion. While gameplay analytics is on, you can copy your Analytics ID in that section and include it in your request. Copy it before turning analytics off, because the game resets the identifier. You can contact us even if the identifier is unavailable. We do not link gameplay events to your email address. When we can identify the relevant records and your deletion request applies, we submit a deletion request to Google Analytics. See the Data deletion instructions.

Required data, automated decisions, and security

The game needs local game data to keep your progress. Payment data is needed only if you make a purchase. You do not need to give a name or email address to play; an email address is needed only if you contact us. The game does not use gameplay analytics to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Google's ad services may select ads automatically under Google's own policies. Our gameplay analytics integration does not send your name or email address. We rely on the security measures of Google Play, Firebase and our other providers.

Age requirement

Hidden in the Canvas is designed for adults, and the minimum permitted age is 16. It is not directed at anyone under 16, and if you are under 16 you must not use it. The game does not ask for or check your age, and it does not tell Google's ad or analytics services your age. Ads are limited to the PG rating, as described above.

If you believe someone under 16 has provided us with personal information, for example by turning on gameplay analytics, accepting advertising choices or contacting us, tell us and we will review what happened and delete the data where appropriate.
