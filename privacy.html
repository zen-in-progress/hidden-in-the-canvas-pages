Privacy Policy

Hidden in the Canvas

This Privacy Policy explains how Hidden in the Canvas handles data when you play the game.

Last updated: June 4, 2026 Contact: hiddeninthecanvas@gmail.com

Short version

The game stores your level progress and purchase state locally or through platform services. If ads are enabled, Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform may process device, advertising, consent, and ad interaction data according to Google's policies.

Who operates the game

Hidden in the Canvas is operated by the developer of the game. You can contact support at hiddeninthecanvas@gmail.com.

Data we handle

  • Gameplay progress, selected level, completed levels, and similar local game state.
  • Remove Ads purchase and restore state through Google Play Billing.
  • Ad consent choices through Google User Messaging Platform.
  • Ad delivery and measurement data through Google AdMob when ads are shown.
  • Technical data needed to run the game, such as device type, app version, and diagnostics from platform services.

Why the data is used

  • To save your progress and keep the game working.
  • To provide and restore the Remove Ads purchase.
  • To show rewarded ads and interstitial ads when ads are enabled.
  • To remember and respect your ad consent choices where required.
  • To improve stability and investigate support requests.

Advertising and consent

Hidden in the Canvas may use Google AdMob to show ads. In regions where consent is required, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage ad consent choices. You can reopen ad consent choices from the game Settings screen.

Google may process data such as advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from network information, device information, ad interaction data, and consent status. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising documentation.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play. The game receives purchase status from Google Play so it can remove ads and restore the entitlement on supported devices. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Children

Hidden in the Canvas is not intended for children under 13. If the game is later distributed as child-directed or family-targeted, the privacy and advertising setup must be reviewed and updated before release.

Your choices

  • You can manage ad consent from Settings inside the game.
  • You can reset or remove local game progress using device/app storage controls.
  • You can restore purchases through the Settings screen.
  • You can contact support to ask privacy questions.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game changes, when new services are added, or when legal requirements change. The latest version will be available on this page.
