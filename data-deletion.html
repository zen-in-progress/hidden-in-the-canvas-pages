Request deletion of gameplay analytics data

  1. In the game, open Settings and scroll to Privacy and support, just above Progress.
  2. If gameplay analytics is enabled, tap Copy next to Analytics ID before turning analytics off or clearing the game's storage. The game resets the identifier when you turn analytics off.
  3. Choose Request data deletion to return to these instructions. Email hiddeninthecanvas@omail.sh and say that you want gameplay analytics data deleted. Include the Analytics ID if you have it. If the request covers more than one installation, include the IDs you have.

You can email us even if the ID is unavailable. We do not link gameplay events to your email address, so we may need more information to find the relevant records. If we can identify the records and your deletion request applies, we can submit a deletion request to Google Analytics. We will explain any limit on what we can identify or delete.

No game account to delete

The game does not offer its own account registration. Your Google Play account is separate and is managed by Google.

Resetting progress inside the game

A progress reset removes the progress covered by that game control. It is not a full deletion of all app data, purchase records, ad data, or data already sent to a provider.

Removing data stored on your Android device

Before clearing data, check your unspent hints and progress. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can permanently remove local progress and unspent purchased hints. The game cannot guarantee restoration of consumable hints afterward.

  1. Open Android Settings, then Apps, then Hidden in the Canvas.
  2. Open Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Android labels vary by device.
  3. If you no longer want the game, uninstall it from Android after clearing storage.

These steps remove data held by the app on that device. They do not cancel or erase Google Play transaction records or data already processed by Google or other providers.

Analytics and advertising choices

To stop future gameplay reporting, turn off Gameplay analytics in Settings. The game clears pending analytics data from your device. For events already received by Google Analytics, follow the request steps above. Where Google makes its advertising privacy form available, you can reopen it through Manage Ad Consent in Settings.

Ask for access or erasure

Email hiddeninthecanvas@omail.sh with the type of request and enough context to locate a support conversation or purchase problem. Do not send a passport, full card number, or other sensitive documents in your first message. We may need limited information to verify a purchase or identify the records relevant to your request, and will explain what is needed.

Some provider events may not be identifiable to a named person from the information available to us. Under GDPR Article 11, we are not required to collect extra identifiers solely to identify a person, but we will assess requests against records we can identify and explain any limitation or legal retention obligation. We cannot promise that a device reset deletes provider-held records.

See the Privacy Policy for your rights, response time, recipients, and the Portuguese data protection authority. If your request concerns a Google Play transaction, Google may also hold separate records under its own policies.
